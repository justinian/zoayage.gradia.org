Ohimaka province, situated between the northern tip of Kamakyora Sound and the
eastern end of Minokyora Bay, is known as the "Gateway Province" for having major
port cities on two separate bodies of water. Ohimaka's combined agricultural
production and trade reach make it one of the Shogunate's most prosperous and
valuable provinces.


### Major Cities

- [Ohimaka City](/pages/Ohimaka_City) is Ohimaka's capitol, and the biggest
    trade port on Mino Bay.
- [Nishifune](/pages/Nishifune) lies at the northern tip of Kamakyora Sound.


### Lord

The previous lord, Meīdo Kagehiro, died in 25 Kirya after succumbing to wounds
inflicted while helping fight off a bandit attack. He is succeeded by his daughter,
Meīdo Mearī.
